# to A.I or Not to A.I, That is the question

The big A.I push by the tech industry has likely touched your life by now.  You have at least heard or experienced something having to do with it.  I use it daily at work in the tech industry and over all I think it helps me do the job.  This is a case of right tool for the right job.

## Enter Gen-A.I for Art and Music

I think the jury is coming back guilty as charged on this front.  People seem to hate it.  I couldn't be happier.  I felt a profound sadness and defeat as a musician and creative person when I found out about Udio and Suno.  I can imagine the reaction was the same for visual artists when they saw how good the generative art was getting. 

To be honest, I didn't understand how they go about training these things.  Just because you are software developer, doesn't mean I automatically understand the entire other domain of ML/AI. As I've learned, I realized that FAANG companies and startups alike were stealing the creative output of all humans who have ever uploaded their art or music to the internet without our concent.  I found something morally repugnant about that. I fundamentally disagree that machines should be used to scrape the humanities.  This is and always should be the domain of human beings.  It is fundamental to our self expression and is the life blood of cultural and understanding. 

## ML as a tool

I kind of don't like that title in the first place.  It's actually just machine learning. It's predicting the next token, it's doing inferential statistics.   It doesn't have the ability of humans to think and feel as we do. All that is smoke and mirrors. Anyone who does attribute sentience to it is either selling you snake oil or trying to entrap you in some cult. 

ML has it's use-cases and what most people don't hear enough about is the breakthroughs that it is enabling.  Fusion plasma containment and disease research through DNA genome mapping being 2 very big ones off the top of my head. 

Instead of focusing on what good we could do with it, of course governments are building a panopticon and companies are looking for the next product they can sell.  

I remember a quote from Jurassic Park.  They were so worried about whether they could, they didn't stop to think about whether they should.  When it comes to the humanities, they really shouldn't have.  In this spirit, I'm seeking visual artists of all types to help me fill out my discography of music with cool human art from deviant art like corners of the inter-webs.  I'm betting it's really not dead yet.

If you are an artist who wants to create some album art, I'll let you pick the song you want to make the art for and I'll pay your fee, provided the fee isn't crazy expensive for a digital print.  You can keep the original if it isn't done digitally to begin with.  I pay your fee and you also get a link back from this site.  It's an act and gesture of mutual support, visual artist to audio artist. 

If you are interested in participating in this, send me an email, instagram or threads message with your targeted tracks that inspired you most and I'll let you know if it is already under commission.  I have a generic album cover I use for tracks that do not yet have album art created.
