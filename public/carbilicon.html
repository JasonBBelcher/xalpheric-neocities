Carbilicon

Noah Richardson - Psytrance and techno producer crafting intricate electronic soundscapes with Korg hardware. Noah doesn't really consider the music he makes to be psychedelic techno-trance, but it is. What we might call psychedelic techno-trance is just 'strange and wonderful' electronic music to him. Known for hypnotic live performances and deep dives into vintage Goa trance culture.

Gear Explorer - Master of Korg Electribe ESX and EMX series, creating tutorials and demonstrations showcasing the depth of classic hardware samplers and synthesizers.
