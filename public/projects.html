How to Use These Files

📱 Koala Sampler

  1. Download the .koala file to your device
  2. Open Koala Sampler app
  3. Tap the folder icon to import
  4. Select the downloaded file
  5. Explore, modify, and learn!

🎓 Learning Tips

  • Study the sequencing patterns
  • Examine sample selection and processing
  • Experiment with effect chains
  • Try remixing with your own samples

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use these in my own tracks?

Yes! You can remix, modify, and incorporate these into your own music. Just provide attribution (credit Xalpheric) and share your derivative work under the same Creative Commons license.

Are the samples included?

Koala Sampler projects (.koala files) contain all samples and settings within the file itself. Everything you need is included.

What if I don't have Koala Sampler?

Koala Sampler is available for iOS and Android. It's an affordable and powerful mobile production tool. These files are specifically designed for Koala.

Will you add more projects?

Absolutely! I'm continuously creating new tracks and will be adding more Koala projects as well as full DAW sessions from Ableton, Logic, and other platforms.
