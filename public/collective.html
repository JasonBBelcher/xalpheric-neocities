01 · COLLECTIVE

MIDI Mob

MIDI Mob Collective is a Birmingham-based music community bringing together producers, musicians, and sonic experimenters. Spanning genres from psytrance to folk, ambient to experimental rock, the collective is united by a love of hardware, vinyl, and the creative process.

Electronic · Koala Sampler · Birmingham AL

Shane and Kaleb Wynn — synth pop, electro punk and post rock through a lo-fi DIY lens. Analog hardware synths and drum machines create a grimy, yet psychedelic garage aesthetic. From Birmingham, Alabama.

Vee / Light-Bleeder

Analog Visual Artist and Electronic Music Producer

LIGHTBLEEDER, aka Vee, is a visual artist from Birmingham, Alabama specializing in analog video art and video feedback synthesis. Capable of making music also, he knows how to blend a variety of sonic genres with visuals to make a better experience for everyone. Live shows, music visualizers, trippy patterns, you name it. Visuals are best described as psychedelic, with a futuristic yet nostalgic feeling.

03 · EVENTS

Live Gatherings

Moments from our live events, jam sessions, and collective gatherings across Birmingham.

NOVEMBER 1ST, 2025

Day of the Dead Vape Loft Street Jam

A full day and night of modular synthesis, live electronic improvisation, and collaborative jamming at Vape Loft in Lakeview. Artists including Will, Whoopsie, Xalpheric, So So, Noah Richardson, and more brought their hardware to the street — Prophet synths, modular rigs, drum machines, and endless patch cables under colorful LED-lit tents.

JUNE 15TH, 2026

Vape Loft Summer Street Jam

It rained most of the day but in the end we prevailed anyway and had a great time. The Birmingham MIDI Mob Collective gathered once again at Vape Loft for another memorable street jam session. Artists including Xalpheric, Lower Hybrid, Hamrock Records, and Noah Richardson, and more brought their hardware to the street — Prophet synths, modular rigs, drum machines, and a PA system with 900w sub-woofer to fill the air with music. The community came together to celebrate creativity, collaboration, and the joy of making music together.
