Lower Hybrid

Shane and Kaleb Wynn - Synth pop, electro punk and post rock projected through a lo-fi DIY lens. Lower Hybrid uses mostly analog hardware synths and drum machines to create a grimy, yet psychedelic garage aesthetic. Minimalist, yet paradoxically expansive. From Birmingham, Alabama.
