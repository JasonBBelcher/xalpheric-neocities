02 · IDENTITY

Birmingham Producer

Xalpheric is a Birmingham-based electronic music producer and co-founder of MidiMob Collective, bringing over 25 years of experience crafting downtempo psyhop, hip-hop, chillhop, and ambient soundscapes. His music blends jazz and blues influences with introspective beats, creating immersive sonic experiences that bridge genres and moods.

Live rig: Roland SP-404 sampler, iPad with Koala Sampler, Yamaha MG10XU mixer, guitar. Vinyl sampling is central — dusty crate-digger identity alongside electronic dancefloor energy.

04 · COLLECTIVE

MIDI Mob

MIDI Mob Collective is a Birmingham-based music community bringing together producers, musicians, and sonic experimenters. Spanning genres from psytrance to folk, ambient to experimental rock, the collective is united by a love of hardware, vinyl, and the creative process.

Carbilicon

Psytrance · Techno · Goa Trance

Noah Richardson — Korg hardware enthusiast and vinyl DJ specializing in classic Goa trance. Known for hypnotic live performances and deep hardware explorations.

Lower Hybrid

Synth Pop · Electro Punk · Post Rock

Shane and Kaleb Wynn — synth pop, electro punk and post rock through a lo-fi DIY lens. Analog hardware synths and drum machines create a grimy, yet psychedelic garage aesthetic.
