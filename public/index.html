W e l c o m e t o X a l p h e r i a

Xalpheric is a Birmingham-based electronic music producer and co-founder of MidiMob Collective, bringing over 25 years of experience crafting downtempo psyhop, hip-hop, chillhop, and ambient soundscapes. His music blends jazz and blues influences with introspective beats, creating immersive sonic experiences that bridge genres and moods.

As a producer, composer, and netlabel artist, Xalpheric has built a rich catalog of work exploring the intersection of hip-hop production, psychedelic textures, and atmospheric electronic music. His deep, reflective approach to sound — shaped by decades of exploration — helps anchor MidiMob's collective vision, inspiring both collaboration and genre-defying sonic experiments.
