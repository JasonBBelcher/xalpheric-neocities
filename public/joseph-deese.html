03 · ABOUT

Hamrock Records

Hamrock Records is a digital recording studio founded by Joseph Deese with a mission to create and share instrumental music designed for mental wellness. Every track is crafted to support listening, relaxation, meditation, and creative inspiration.

Based in Birmingham, Alabama, the project began as a personal outlet and has grown into a vast collection of over 955 instrumental tracks distributed across SoundCloud and other platforms. The philosophy is simple: music as a tool for mental health and well-being.

Whether you're looking for background ambience, meditation soundscapes, focus music, or pure creative listening, Hamrock Records offers something for everyone. Each track is an invitation to pause, breathe, and find your own peace.

For inquiries or support, contact hamrockrecords@gmail.com
